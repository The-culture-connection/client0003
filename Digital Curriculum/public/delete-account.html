Delete your MORTAR Alumni Network account and data

This page explains how to permanently delete your account and associated data from MORTAR Alumni Network (also shown as THE MORTARVERSE on iOS) and the MORTAR web learning platform. You do not need to be signed in, or to have the app installed, to request deletion.

How to request deletion

Use whichever of these suits you. All three reach the same team.

From inside the app

  1. Open MORTAR Alumni Network and go to your profile.
  2. Tap Edit profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, then confirm.

On the web, without the app

Fill in the request form with the email address on your account. We email you to confirm it is really you before anything is deleted.

Open the deletion request form Copy our support email

Or write to us directly at masters@wearemortar.com.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, sign-in credentials, and profile, including your photo
  • Posts, comments, replies, and discussion threads you created
  • Direct messages you sent, and your group memberships
  • Event, conference, and session registrations, and check-in history
  • Badges, missions, and course or quiz progress
  • Your networking profile, matches, and connection activity
  • Usage analytics linked to your account

What we keep, and why

  • Purchase and payment records — retained where we are legally required to keep financial records for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Safety and moderation records — retained where needed to resolve a dispute, investigate a report, or enforce our terms.
  • Aggregated statistics — totals and counts that no longer identify you, and cannot be traced back to you once your account is gone.
Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We complete requests within 30 days of confirming your identity. Retained records listed above are kept only for as long as the legal or safety purpose requires, and are then deleted.
