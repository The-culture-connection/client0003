# Section: 4.2 R.E.S.P.E.C.T

## Type
Psychology + Customer Needs Analysis

## Core Concept
Businesses succeed when they understand human motivations, emotional needs, psychological drivers, and customer fulfillment patterns.

## Educational Topic
Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs

## Historical Reference
Introduced by psychologist Abraham Maslow in 1943.

## Main Teaching Points

### Entrepreneurs and Customers Both Have Needs
Every entrepreneur and customer has internal motivations, emotional drivers, unmet needs, and psychological priorities.

### Self-Awareness Is Critical
Entrepreneurs should understand why they make decisions, what motivates them, and what motivates customers.

### Customer Purchases Satisfy Needs
Products/services often satisfy safety, belonging, esteem, self-fulfillment, and emotional connection.

## Visual Aid
Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs pyramid (Physiological, Safety, Love/Belonging, Self-Esteem, Self-Actualization).

# Section: 4.4 The Customer's Always Right

## Type
Customer Discovery + Market Segmentation

## Main Heading
The customer's always right.

## Core Teaching Points
A business is validated when customers pay, return, and find value. A business with no customers is out of business.

## Highlight Callout

### Title
Your customer is NOT everyone

### Supporting Text
Successful businesses identify a specific target customer.

### Main Reflection Question
Who is your target customer?

## Demographic Segmentation
Gender, age, income level, neighborhood, education, culture/community.

## Wants, Needs, or Problems
Customers spend money when a problem matters enough and perceived value is high.

## Price Point
A product is only worth what customers are willing to pay.

## Customer Personas
Create customer profiles for 1–2 current or ideal customers. Identify 5–10 shared traits.

Assignment Submission
