1Mortarverse — current screen + three action-first redesigns

What I read. mortarverse_chooser_screen.dart (staging) — black radial-gradient starfield, 60 seeded stars, centered THE MORTARVERSE + “Explore the digital neighborhood”, then three 260px-wide storefront cards stacked with 28px gaps, each tinting assets/conference/shop_storefront.png to its shop colour with an OPEN NOW / LOCKED pill. Colours from app_theme.dart (#C1121F), conference_colors.dart (#E6DBB4), commons_colors.dart (#A8A8A8).

The problem. On a 390×844 device the third tile falls below the fold, all three tiles look equally “open” whether or not anything is happening, and the screen answers “where can I go?” but never “what needs me?”. Every redesign below keeps the storefront metaphor and the exact per-shop palette, shrinks the tiles so all three fit above the fold, and puts a live action layer on top. Prompts stay direct and functional; the quiet-day slot is a suggested action (finish profile / browse members). QR scanning is a persistent FAB in all three.
