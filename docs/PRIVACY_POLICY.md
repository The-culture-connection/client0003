## 2. Information we collect

### a. Information you provide

- **Account & access:** email address and password (managed by our authentication provider), and, for invite-based access, your invitation eligibility (email on an approved roster) and cohort.
- **Profile:** first and last name, display name, city and state, cohort, biography, profile photo, business/logo image, professional goals, skills you’re confident in, skills you want to develop, industry, and links you choose to add (e.g. LinkedIn, portfolio, and other social profiles).
- **Content you create:** posts, community/group threads and comments, replies, votes/likes, direct messages, event submissions and RSVPs, job and skill listings, survey responses, and graduation/alumni applications.
- **Communications & preferences:** messages you send us, and your email notification preferences (e.g. events, course nudges, admin messages).
- **Support & reports:** information you submit when you contact us or report content or another user.

### b. Payment information

When you make a purchase (e.g. paid course modules, event tickets, or shop orders), payment card details are collected and processed **directly by our payment processor, Stripe** — we do **not** receive or store your full card number. We retain order records such as items purchased, amounts, tax, shipping address (for shop orders), and payment status.

### c. Information collected automatically

- **Usage & analytics:** in-app events and interactions (e.g. screens viewed, features used, checkout steps), timestamps, and a session identifier, used to understand and improve the Services.
- **Device & technical data:** device and app version information, and a **push-notification token** if you enable notifications, so we can deliver alerts.
- **We do not collect precise GPS location.** Any location shown (e.g. your city/state) is text you provide.

### d. Information from third parties

- **Authentication and infrastructure providers** (see Section 6) supply account and technical data needed to operate the Services.
- If your organization or a cohort administrator adds you to an approved access roster, we receive the email address used to invite you.

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account and verify eligibility/access;
- Provide the Services — profiles, communities, events, direct messaging, the jobs & skills marketplace, and **matching** (suggesting relevant members based on your skills, goals, and industry);
- Process payments and fulfill orders;
- Send transactional and, where permitted, informational or promotional emails and push notifications (you can opt out — see Section 8);
- Personalize content and recommendations;
- Maintain safety and integrity — moderate content, review reports, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our Terms;
- Analyze and improve the Services and develop new features;
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

**Legal bases (EEA/UK users):** we process your information to perform our contract with you (providing the Services), based on your consent (e.g. marketing emails, push notifications), for our legitimate interests (securing and improving the Services), and to comply with legal obligations.

## 4. How your information is shared / visible

- **With other users:** your profile (name, photo, skills, goals, industry, links) and the content you post (community threads, comments, listings, event participation) are visible to other members of the network. Direct messages are visible to the participants of that conversation. Please don’t share anything you consider private in public areas of the Services.
- **With administrators:** [ENTITY SHORT] staff/administrators can view member profiles, content, reports, and order/payment records to operate and moderate the Services.
- **With service providers (processors):** see Section 6.
- **For legal reasons:** to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, or protect the rights, safety, and property of [ENTITY SHORT], our users, or others.
- **Business transfers:** in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

**We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.**

## 5. Community content and messaging

Content you post to communities, events, listings, and discussions may be seen by other members and by administrators, and may remain visible to others even after you delete your account (for example, in others’ conversations or where a copy has been re-shared). Direct messages are accessible to the people in the conversation and to administrators for safety and moderation.
